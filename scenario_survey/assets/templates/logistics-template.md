# Logistics Industry Survey Questionnaire (English)

> Target Industry: Logistics/Supply Chain
> Survey Mode: Interview/Self-reported
> Language: English (Default)

---

## Part 1: Basic Information (Opening warm-up)

1. Could you briefly introduce your company?
   - Main logistics business? (Warehousing, transport, supply chain management)
   - Number of locations? Countries?
   - Staff count? Main client types?

2. What's the background for considering this project?
   - When did you start thinking about this?
   - What timeline do you expect?

3. What's the project name or theme?
   - Is there a formal project name?
   - How do people usually refer to this project?

## Part 3: Success Criteria & Expectations (Core value)

10. What solutions or approaches are you considering?
    [Follow-up] Why would you choose one over others?
    [Follow-up] What advantages compared to alternatives?

11. What factors would matter most in your decision?
    [Ranking guidance] Most important? Second? Third?
    [Follow-up] Why is this most important?

[Logistics Industry Common Factors]
Typical focus areas in logistics include:
- Business continuity (network cannot interrupt, supply chain stable)
- Global unification (multi-country unified standards)
- Data security (client information protection, compliance)
- Remote management (headquarters monitors all locations)
- Compliance certification (TISAX and other standards)

12. If the most important factor wasn't achieved, what would that mean?
    [Follow-up] Would project still be successful?
    [Follow-up] Which are "must achieve" vs "should achieve"?

13. What would success look like for each factor?
    [Follow-up] Current network interruption frequency? Target?
    [Follow-up] Current remote management approach? Target improvement?
    [Follow-up] Current security incidents? Target reduction?
    [Estimation guidance] Rough estimates are fine

## Part 4: Stakeholders (Participant panorama)

[Organization Layer Guidance]
14. Let's walk through who would be involved:
    - Who would approve? (CEO, board)
    - Who would manage execution? (VP IT, IT director)
    - Which locations/countries would participate? (Start from which)
    - Who would use daily? (Warehouse staff, drivers, dispatchers)
    - Who else cares? (Clients, partners, regulators)

[Four-Party Interest Guidance]
15. Think about these four aspects:
    - Who would benefit? (Operations stable, IT efficient)
    - Whose work would change? (Warehouse methods, dispatcher operations)
    - Who would actively support? (IT lead, operations lead)
    - Who might have concerns? (Local operations independence, cost)

[External Connection Guidance]
16. Besides internal staff, external parties?
    - Client requirements (automotive etc.)? (On-time delivery, information security)
    - Partners connectivity needs?
    - Regulatory compliance? (TISAX etc.)

17. What would each expect?
    [Follow-up] Operations staff care about? (Stable, efficient)
    [Follow-up] IT team wants? (Remote management, unified standards)
    [Follow-up] Clients care about? (Service continuity, data security)
    [Follow-up] Management cares about? (Compliance, cost, client satisfaction)
