# Retail Industry Survey Questionnaire (English)

> Target Industry: Retail
> Survey Mode: Interview/Self-reported
> Language: English (Default)

---

## Part 1: Basic Information (Opening warm-up)

1. Could you briefly introduce your company?
   - Main retail business? (Online, offline, omnichannel)
   - Number of stores? Regions?
   - Staff count? Main customer segments?

2. What's the background for considering this project?
   - When did you start thinking about this?
   - What timeline do you expect?

3. What's the project name or theme?
   - Is there a formal project name?
   - How do people usually refer to this project?

## Part 3: Success Criteria & Expectations (Core value)

10. What solutions or approaches are you considering?
    [Follow-up] Why would you choose one over others?
    [Follow-up] What advantages compared to alternatives?

11. What factors would matter most in your decision?
    [Ranking guidance] Most important? Second? Third?
    [Follow-up] Why is this most important?

[Retail Industry Common Factors]
Typical focus areas in retail include:
- Customer experience (store Wi-Fi, smooth service)
- Operations efficiency (POS system, inventory management)
- Logistics efficiency (warehouse network, picking efficiency)
- Multi-location consistency (all stores same experience)
- Rating impact (customer reviews, online ratings)

12. If the most important factor wasn't achieved, what would that mean?
    [Follow-up] Would project still be successful?
    [Follow-up] Which are "must achieve" vs "should achieve"?

13. What would success look like for each factor?
    [Follow-up] Current store network experience? Target?
    [Follow-up] Current customer complaints? Target reduction?
    [Follow-up] Current inventory management efficiency? Target?
    [Estimation guidance] Rough estimates are fine

## Part 4: Stakeholders (Participant panorama)

[Organization Layer Guidance]
14. Let's walk through who would be involved:
    - Who would approve? (CEO, board)
    - Who would manage execution? (IT director, operations director)
    - Which stores/warehouses would participate? (Start from which)
    - Who would use daily? (Store staff, warehouse staff, customers)
    - Who else cares? (Suppliers, rating platforms)

[Four-Party Interest Guidance]
15. Think about these four aspects:
    - Who would benefit? (Customer experience, staff efficiency)
    - Whose work would change? (Store staff, warehouse staff)
    - Who would actively support? (IT lead, operations lead)
    - Who might have concerns? (Cost, store change)

[External Connection Guidance]
16. Besides internal staff, external parties?
    - Customer requirements? (Smooth shopping, Wi-Fi convenience)
    - Supplier connectivity needs?
    - Rating platform influence? (Trustpilot etc.)

17. What would each expect?
    [Follow-up] Customers care about? (Smooth shopping, free Wi-Fi)
    [Follow-up] Store staff care about? (POS stable, inventory visibility)
    [Follow-up] Warehouse staff care about? (Picking efficiency, coverage)
    [Follow-up] Management cares about? (Sales, ratings, cost)
