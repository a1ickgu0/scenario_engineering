# Services Industry Survey Questionnaire (English)

> Target Industry: Professional Services (Legal, Consulting, IT Services, Accounting, etc.)
> Survey Mode: Interview/Self-reported
> Language: English (Default)

---

## Part 1: Basic Information (Opening warm-up)

1. Could you briefly introduce your company?
   - Main services business? (Legal, consulting, IT services, accounting, etc.)
   - Number of offices? Regions/countries?
   - Staff count? Main client types?

2. What's the background for considering this project?
   - When did you start thinking about this?
   - What timeline do you expect?

3. What's the project name or theme?
   - Is there a formal project name?
   - How do people usually refer to this project?

---

## Part 2: Current Challenges (Story starting point)

4. What challenges are you currently facing?
   [Follow-up] What specifically does this look like? Examples?
   [Follow-up] Which challenge is most concerning?

5. Professional staff work challenges?
   [Follow-up] Remote access to client data convenient?
   [Follow-up] Mobile office support?
   [Follow-up] Collaboration tools smooth?

6. Client service challenges?
   [Follow-up] Client communication tools stable?
   [Follow-up] Project collaboration platform issues?
   [Follow-up] Data sharing secure?

7. Data security concerns?
   [Follow-up] Client data protection requirements?
   [Follow-up] Compliance audit issues?
   [Follow-up] Remote access secure?

8. Impact on services business?
   [Follow-up] If not addressed, what would happen?
   [Follow-up] Can you quantify? (efficiency, client satisfaction)

9. Have you tried other approaches before?
   [Follow-up] How did that work out?
   [Follow-up] Why didn't those solve the problem?

---

## Part 3: Success Criteria & Expectations (Core value)

10. What solutions or approaches are you considering?
    [Follow-up] Why would you choose one over others?
    [Follow-up] What advantages compared to alternatives?

11. What factors would matter most in your decision?
    [Ranking guidance] Most important? Second? Third?
    [Follow-up] Why is this most important?

[Services Industry Common Factors]
Typical focus areas include:
- Professional efficiency (remote access, mobile office)
- Client collaboration (smooth communication, data sharing)
- Data security (client data protection, compliance)
- Multi-office consistency (all offices same experience)
- IT efficiency (centralized management)

12. If the most important factor wasn't achieved, what would that mean?
    [Follow-up] Would project still be successful?
    [Follow-up] Which are "must achieve" vs "should achieve"?

13. What would success look like for each factor?
    [Follow-up] Current remote access efficiency? Target?
    [Follow-up] Current collaboration efficiency? Target?
    [Follow-up] Current security incidents? Target reduction?
    [Estimation guidance] Rough estimates are fine

## Part 4: Stakeholders (Participant panorama)

[Organization Layer Guidance]
14. Let's walk through who would be involved:
    - Who would approve? (Partners, board)
    - Who would manage execution? (IT director, IT manager)
    - Which offices/teams would participate? (Start from which)
    - Who would use daily? (Professionals, assistants, clients)
    - Who else cares? (Clients, regulators)

[Four-Party Interest Guidance]
15. Think about these four aspects:
    - Who would benefit? (Professional efficiency, client experience)
    - Whose work would change? (Work methods, collaboration methods)
    - Who would actively support? (IT lead, operations lead)
    - Who might have concerns? (Cost, change)

[External Connection Guidance]
16. Besides internal staff, external parties?
    - Client requirements? (Data security, communication convenience)
    - Partner connectivity needs?
    - Regulatory compliance?

17. What would each expect?
    [Follow-up] Professionals care about? (Efficiency, convenience)
    [Follow-up] Assistants care about? (Collaboration smooth)
    [Follow-up] Clients care about? (Quick response, data security)
    [Follow-up] Management cares about? (Compliance, cost, efficiency)
