# Manufacturing Industry Survey Questionnaire (English)

> Target Industry: Manufacturing
> Survey Mode: Interview/Self-reported
> Language: English (Default)

---

## Part 1: Basic Information (Opening warm-up)

1. Could you briefly introduce your company?
   - Main manufacturing business? (Components, equipment, consumer goods)
   - Number of factories/production sites? Countries?
   - Staff count? Main client types?

2. What's the background for considering this project?
   - When did you start thinking about this?
   - What timeline do you expect?

3. What's the project name or theme?
   - Is there a formal project name?
   - How do people usually refer to this project?

---

## Part 2: Current Challenges (Story starting point)

4. What challenges are you currently facing?
   [Follow-up] What specifically does this look like? Examples?
   [Follow-up] Which challenge is most concerning?

5. Production operations challenges?
   [Follow-up] Factory network stable? Affects production?
   [Follow-up] Equipment connectivity issues? (IoT sensors, machine monitoring)
   [Follow-up] Predictive maintenance support?

6. Data security concerns?
   [Follow-up] Production data protection requirements?
   [Follow-up] Cyber attacks experienced?
   [Follow-up] Industry compliance requirements? (TISAX etc.)

7. Global operations challenges?
   [Follow-up] Cross-factory connections stable?
   [Follow-up] Headquarters monitoring each factory?
   [Follow-up] Remote management difficulties?

8. Impact on production business?
   [Follow-up] If not addressed, what would happen?
   [Follow-up] Can you quantify? (downtime losses, compliance risk, efficiency)

9. Have you tried other approaches before?
   [Follow-up] How did that work out?
   [Follow-up] Why didn't those solve the problem?

## Part 3: Success Criteria & Expectations (Core value)

10. What solutions or approaches are you considering?
    [Follow-up] Why would you choose one over others?
    [Follow-up] What advantages compared to alternatives?

11. What factors would matter most in your decision?
    [Ranking guidance] Most important? Second? Third?
    [Follow-up] Why is this most important?

[Manufacturing Industry Common Factors]
Typical focus areas in manufacturing include:
- Production efficiency (network stable, equipment connectivity)
- Predictive maintenance (IoT data collection, failure prediction)
- Data security (production data protection, compliance)
- Global unification (multi-factory unified standards)
- Compliance certification (TISAX and other standards)

12. If the most important factor wasn't achieved, what would that mean?
    [Follow-up] Would project still be successful?
    [Follow-up] Which are "must achieve" vs "should achieve"?

13. What would success look like for each factor?
    [Follow-up] Current production network stability? Target?
    [Follow-up] Current equipment failure prediction? Target improvement?
    [Follow-up] Current security incidents? Target reduction?
    [Estimation guidance] Rough estimates are fine

## Part 4: Stakeholders (Participant panorama)

[Organization Layer Guidance]
14. Let's walk through who would be involved:
    - Who would approve? (CEO, board)
    - Who would manage execution? (VP IT, plant manager)
    - Which factories would participate? (Start from which)
    - Who would use daily? (Production workers, maintenance, dispatchers)
    - Who else cares? (Clients, suppliers, regulators)

[Four-Party Interest Guidance]
15. Think about these four aspects:
    - Who would benefit? (Production stable, maintenance efficient)
    - Whose work would change? (Production methods, maintenance process)
    - Who would actively support? (IT lead, plant manager)
    - Who might have concerns? (Local independence, cost)

[External Connection Guidance]
16. Besides internal staff, external parties?
    - Client requirements (automotive etc.)? (Quality standards, information security)
    - Supplier connectivity needs?
    - Regulatory compliance?

17. What would each expect?
    [Follow-up] Production staff care about? (Stable, efficient)
    [Follow-up] Maintenance staff care about? (Prediction, convenience)
    [Follow-up] Management cares about? (Compliance, cost, efficiency)
