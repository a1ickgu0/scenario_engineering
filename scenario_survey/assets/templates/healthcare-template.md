# Healthcare Industry Survey Questionnaire (English)

> Target Industry: Healthcare (Hospitals/Clinics/Medical Groups)
> Survey Mode: Interview/Self-reported
> Language: English (Default)

---

## Part 1: Basic Information (Opening warm-up)

1. Could you briefly introduce your healthcare organization?
   - Hospital, clinic, or medical group? Public or private?
   - Number of facilities/campuses? Bed count? Staff count?
   - Service area? Patient population served?

2. What's the background for considering this project?
   - When did you start thinking about this?
   - What timeline do you expect?

3. What's the project name or theme?
   - Is there a formal project name?
   - How do people usually refer to this project?

---

## Part 2: Current Challenges (Story starting point)

4. What challenges are you currently facing?
   [Follow-up] What specifically does this look like? Examples?
   [Follow-up] Which challenge is most concerning?

5. What difficulties do clinical staff face currently?
   [Follow-up] Accessing patient information? What problems?
   [Follow-up] Mobile rounds - network stable?
   [Follow-up] Remote diagnosis or community services - limitations?

6. Patient service challenges?
   [Follow-up] Patient wait times? Appointment booking?
   [Follow-up] Remote consultations supported?

7. Data security concerns?
   [Follow-up] Patient privacy protection requirements?
   [Follow-up] Data breach risks?
   [Follow-up] Compliance audit issues?

8. What impact on healthcare operations?
   [Follow-up] If not addressed, what would happen?
   [Follow-up] Can you quantify? (wait times, efficiency, compliance risk)

9. Have you tried other approaches before?
   [Follow-up] How did that work out?
   [Follow-up] Why didn't those solve the problem?

## Part 3: Success Criteria & Expectations (Core value)

10. What solutions or approaches are you considering?
    [Follow-up] Why would you choose one over others?
    [Follow-up] What advantages compared to alternatives?

11. What factors would matter most in your decision?
    [Ranking guidance] Most important? Second? Third?
    [Follow-up] Why is this most important?

[Healthcare Industry Common Factors]
Typical focus areas in healthcare include:
- Patient data security (privacy protection, compliance)
- Clinical efficiency (mobile rounds, fast record access)
- Remote healthcare (community services, remote diagnosis)
- System reliability (no interruptions, 24x7 operation)
- Compliance certification (industry standards, regulatory)

12. If the most important factor wasn't achieved, what would that mean?
    [Follow-up] Would project still be successful?
    [Follow-up] Which are "must achieve" vs "should achieve"?

13. What would success look like for each factor?
    [Follow-up] Current information access time? Target?
    [Follow-up] Current patient wait times? Target reduction?
    [Follow-up] Remote diagnosis support current? Expected?
    [Estimation guidance] Rough estimates are fine

## Part 4: Stakeholders (Participant panorama)

[Organization Layer Guidance]
14. Let's walk through who would be involved:
    - Who would approve? (CEO, board, health authority)
    - Who would manage execution? (CIO, IT director, IT department)
    - Which departments would participate? (Clinical, nursing, outpatient)
    - Who would use daily? (Doctors, nurses, patients)
    - Who else cares? (Health authority, insurance, patient families)

[Four-Party Interest Guidance]
15. Think about these four aspects:
    - Who would benefit? (Clinical efficiency, patient experience)
    - Whose work would change? (Doctor rounds, nurse documentation)
    - Who would actively support? (IT, clinical department leads)
    - Who might have concerns? (Department change concerns, cost)

[External Connection Guidance]
16. Besides internal staff, external parties?
    - Patient requirements? (Privacy, convenience)
    - Regulatory bodies (health authority, insurance) requirements?
    - Partner hospitals/community clinics - connectivity needs?

17. What would each expect?
    [Follow-up] Doctors care about? (Fast info access, mobile convenience)
    [Follow-up] Nurses care about? (Easy documentation, less manual)
    [Follow-up] Patients care about? (Privacy, convenience)
    [Follow-up] Management cares about? (Compliance, efficiency, cost)

## Part 5: Expected Usage Scenarios (Usage details)

["A Day's Work" - Doctor Perspective]
18. Let's imagine a doctor's typical day:
    - Morning rounds - how would system be used? What devices?
    - During outpatient/surgery - accessing patient information?
    - Before leaving - any system needs?
    - Night shift - remote access needs?

["A Day's Work" - Nurse Perspective]
19. How would nurses use the system daily?
    - Nursing documentation? Mobile or fixed devices?
    - Patient monitoring data transmission?
    - Medication management system?

["A Day's Work" - Patient Perspective]
20. Patient interaction with systems?
    - Appointment booking?
    - Queue/call system at facility?
    - Viewing test results?

[Role Perspective Switch]
21. Different roles different usage?
    [Follow-up] Clinical doctors? (Diagnosis, prescriptions, rounds)
    [Follow-up] Outpatient doctors? (Quick consultation, appointments)
    [Follow-up] Community doctors? (Home visits, remote connection)
    [Follow-up] Administrative staff? (Management, statistics, compliance)

[Detail Drill]
22. Describe doctor rounds operation?
    [Follow-up] What devices? Tablet or phone?
    [Follow-up] How get patient info? Scan or direct open?
    [Follow-up] Ward network coverage? Signal stable?
    [Follow-up] Video calls during rounds?

[Remote Healthcare]
23. Remote diagnosis or community services?
    [Follow-up] Doctor remotely viewing images?
    [Follow-up] Community nurse home visits - devices?
    [Follow-up] Patient home connection with doctor?

[Peak Periods]
24. When is network most used?
    [Follow-up] Outpatient peak hours? Concurrent users?
    [Follow-up] Emergency or night special needs?
    [Follow-up] Can system handle? Ever congested?

[Exception Cases]
25. Special situations:
    - If network fails, can clinical operations continue? Backup plan?
    - If patient data system fails, emergency process?
    - Any unexpected situations? How handled?
