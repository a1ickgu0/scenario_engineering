# Education Industry Survey Questionnaire (English)

> Target Industry: Education (K-12/Higher Education)
> Survey Mode: Interview/Self-reported
> Language: English (Default)

---

## Part 1: Basic Information (Opening warm-up)

1. Could you briefly introduce your school/institution?
   - K-12 school or university? Public or private?
   - Student count? Staff count?
   - Number of campuses? Campus area?

2. What's the background for considering this project?
   - When did you start thinking about this?
   - What timeline do you expect?

3. What's the project name or theme?
   - Is there a formal project name?
   - How do people usually refer to this project?

---

## Part 2: Current Challenges (Story starting point)

4. What challenges are you currently facing?
   [Follow-up] What specifically does this look like? Examples?
   [Follow-up] Which challenge is most concerning?

5. Student learning experience challenges?
   [Follow-up] Classroom network stable? Students can browse smoothly?
   [Follow-up] 1:1 device policy execution difficulties? (if applicable)
   [Follow-up] Online learning/remote teaching support?

6. Teacher teaching challenges?
   [Follow-up] Digital tools in classroom smooth?
   [Follow-up] Lesson prep, grading systems? What problems?
   [Follow-up] Mobile teaching support?

7. Campus coverage challenges?
   [Follow-up] Which areas have poor network? (Classrooms, dorms, library, fields)
   [Follow-up] Network slow when crowded?
   [Follow-up] Building connectivity?

8. Impact on education?
   [Follow-up] If not addressed, what would happen?
   [Follow-up] Can you quantify? (learning efficiency, teaching convenience)

9. Have you tried other approaches before?
   [Follow-up] How did that work out?
   [Follow-up] Why didn't those solve the problem?

---

## Part 3: Success Criteria & Expectations (Core value)

10. What solutions or approaches are you considering?
    [Follow-up] Why would you choose one over others?
    [Follow-up] What advantages compared to alternatives?

11. What factors would matter most in your decision?
    [Ranking guidance] Most important? Second? Third?
    [Follow-up] Why is this most important?

[Education Industry Common Factors]
Typical focus areas in education include:
- Student experience (stable network, full coverage)
- Teaching support (digital classroom, online learning)
- Device coverage (1:1 policy, student devices)
- Safety compliance (student data protection, content filtering)
- IT efficiency (centralized management)

12. If the most important factor wasn't achieved, what would that mean?
    [Follow-up] Would project still be successful?
    [Follow-up] Which are "must achieve" vs "should achieve"?

13. What would success look like for each factor?
    [Follow-up] Current classroom network experience? Target?
    [Follow-up] Current coverage? Target students?
    [Follow-up] Current network issues resolution? Target time?
    [Estimation guidance] Rough estimates are fine

## Part 4: Stakeholders (Participant panorama)

[Organization Layer Guidance]
14. Let's walk through who would be involved:
    - Who would approve? (Principal, board, education authority)
    - Who would manage execution? (IT director, IT manager)
    - Which departments would participate? (Academic departments, faculties)
    - Who would use daily? (Students, teachers, staff)
    - Who else cares? (Parents, education authority, alumni)

[Four-Party Interest Guidance]
15. Think about these four aspects:
    - Who would benefit? (Student learning convenience, teacher teaching convenience)
    - Whose work would change? (Teacher methods, student learning methods)
    - Who would actively support? (IT lead, academic lead)
    - Who might have concerns? (Cost concerns, change concerns)

[External Connection Guidance]
16. Besides internal staff, external parties?
    - Parent requirements? (Understanding student learning, communication)
    - Education authority requirements? (1:1 policy, data security)
    - Partner schools/educational institutions?

17. What would each expect?
    [Follow-up] Students care about? (Network stable, fast)
    [Follow-up] Teachers care about? (Teaching convenience, prep efficiency)
    [Follow-up] Parents care about? (Learning visibility, data security)
    [Follow-up] Management cares about? (Compliance, cost, reputation)
