# Hospitality Industry Survey Questionnaire (English)

> Target Industry: Hospitality (Hotels/Tourism/Accommodation)
> Survey Mode: Interview/Self-reported
> Language: English (Default)

---

## Part 1: Basic Information (Opening warm-up)

1. Could you briefly introduce your hotel/group?
   - Single property or chain? How many properties?
   - Room count? Staff count?
   - Market positioning? (Economy, Business, Luxury)

2. What's the background for considering this project?
   - When did you start thinking about this?
   - What timeline do you expect?

3. What's the project name or theme?
   - Is there a formal project name?
   - How do people usually refer to this project?

---

## Part 2: Current Challenges (Story starting point)

4. What challenges are you currently facing?
   [Follow-up] What specifically does this look like? Examples?
   [Follow-up] Which challenge is most concerning?

5. What feedback do guests currently give?
   [Follow-up] Most common complaints? (Network issues, coverage, speed)
   [Follow-up] Any specific cases? Like "can't connect to Wi-Fi"?
   [Follow-up] Impact on ratings or reputation?

6. Is guest experience consistent across properties?
   [Follow-up] Guests notice differences between properties?
   [Follow-up] How do you currently manage network at each property?
   [Follow-up] Any brand standard requirements?

7. What impact are these challenges having?
   [Follow-up] If not addressed, what would happen?
   [Follow-up] Can you quantify? (complaints, ratings, occupancy)

8. Have you tried other approaches before?
   [Follow-up] How did that work out?
   [Follow-up] Why didn't those solve the problem?

---

## Part 3: Success Criteria & Expectations (Core value)

9. What solutions or approaches are you considering?
   [Follow-up] Why would you choose one over others?
   [Follow-up] What advantages compared to alternatives?

10. What factors would matter most in your decision?
    [Ranking guidance] Most important? Second? Third?
    [Follow-up] Why is this most important?

[Hospitality Industry Common Factors]
Typical focus areas in hospitality include:
- Guest experience (Wi-Fi stability, complimentary)
- Multi-property consistency (uniform experience)
- IT efficiency (remote management, reduced travel)
- Cost control (WAN costs, operational costs)
- Rating impact (star ratings, online reviews)

11. If the most important factor wasn't achieved, what would that mean?
    [Follow-up] Would project still be successful?
    [Follow-up] Which are "must achieve" vs "should achieve"?

12. What would success look like for each factor?
    [Follow-up] Current guest complaints? Target reduction?
    [Follow-up] Current issue resolution time? Target?
    [Follow-up] Current WAN costs? Target reduction?
    [Estimation guidance] Rough estimates are fine

---

## Part 4: Stakeholders (Participant panorama)

[Organization Layer Guidance]
13. Let's walk through who would be involved:
    - Who would approve this? (Group leadership, board, investors)
    - Who would manage execution? (IT Director, IT Manager)
    - Which properties would participate first?
    - Who would use daily? (Guests, front desk, housekeeping)
    - Who else cares? (Brand owner, franchise partners, rating platforms)

[Four-Party Interest Guidance]
14. Think about these four aspects:
    - Who would benefit? (Guests, IT team)
    - Whose work would change? (Front desk, housekeeping, IT)
    - Who would actively support? (IT lead, operations director)
    - Who might have concerns? (Finance cost concerns, property independence)

[External Connection Guidance]
15. Besides hotel staff, external parties?
    - Guest requirements? Network ratings matter?
    - Rating platforms (TripAdvisor, Booking) influence?
    - Brand/franchise standards?

16. What would each expect?
    [Follow-up] Guests care about? (Free Wi-Fi? Stable? Fast?)
    [Follow-up] IT team wants? (Remote management? Less travel?)
    [Follow-up] Management cares about? (Cost? Brand image?)

---

## Part 5: Expected Usage Scenarios (Usage details)

["A Day's Work" - Guest Perspective]
17. Let's imagine a guest's experience from arrival to departure:
    - How would guests connect at arrival?
    - Would they use it in room, restaurant, meeting spaces?
    - Evening video streaming, video calls experience?
    - Before leaving, any network needs?

["A Day's Work" - Staff Perspective]
18. How would hotel staff use the network?
    - Front desk system needs? Network requirements?
    - Housekeeping mobile devices?
    - Restaurant/meeting booking systems?
    - IT monitoring and management?

[Role Perspective Switch]
19. Different guest types?
    [Follow-up] Business guests? (Video calls, VPN, email)
    [Follow-up] Leisure guests? (Streaming, social media)
    [Follow-up] Meeting/event groups? (Many concurrent users)

[Detail Drill]
20. Describe guest Wi-Fi connection steps?
    [Follow-up] First step? Auto-connect or password?
    [Follow-up] Different devices (phone, laptop, tablet)?
    [Follow-up] Connection speed? Video streaming capability?

[Peak Periods]
21. When would network be most used?
    [Follow-up] Large events/meetings? How many concurrent users?
    [Follow-up] Holiday/peak season pressure?
    [Follow-up] Can system handle it? Ever had congestion?

[Exception Cases]
22. Special situations:
    - If network fails, can front desk still operate? Backup plan?
    - If area has poor signal, how handle guest complaints?
    - Any unexpected situations? How handled?

    [Follow-up] Industry trend toward complimentary?

27. Current network equipment?

    [Follow-up] Limitations?
    [Follow-up] Replace or upgrade?

28. Current WAN situation?
    [Follow-up] MPLS? Regular broadband?
    [Follow-up] Costs? Consider alternatives?
