# Sports & Entertainment Industry Survey Questionnaire (English)

> Target Industry: Sports & Entertainment
> Survey Mode: Interview/Self-reported
> Language: English (Default)

---

## Part 1: Basic Information (Opening warm-up)

1. Could you briefly introduce your organization?
   - Sports club, event organizer, or entertainment venue?
   - Venue capacity? Seat count?
   - Staff count? Main audience/fan groups?

2. What's the background for considering this project?
   - When did you start thinking about this?
   - What timeline do you expect?

3. What's the project name or theme?
   - Is there a formal project name?
   - How do people usually refer to this project?

## Part 3: Success Criteria & Expectations (Core value)

10. What solutions or approaches are you considering?
    [Follow-up] Why would you choose one over others?
    [Follow-up] What advantages compared to alternatives?

11. What factors would matter most in your decision?
    [Ranking guidance] Most important? Second? Third?
    [Follow-up] Why is this most important?

[Sports & Entertainment Common Factors]
Typical focus areas include:
- Audience experience (live network, social sharing)
- Peak capacity (tens of thousands concurrent users)
- Data insights (audience behavior, athlete data)
- Operations efficiency (ticketing, security, logistics)
- Commercial value (sponsorship, advertising, fan engagement)

12. If the most important factor wasn't achieved, what would that mean?
    [Follow-up] Would project still be successful?
    [Follow-up] Which are "must achieve" vs "should achieve"?

13. What would success look like for each factor?
    [Follow-up] Current live network experience? Target?
    [Follow-up] Current peak handling? Target improvement?
    [Follow-up] Current data collection capability? Target?
    [Estimation guidance] Rough estimates are fine

## Part 4: Stakeholders (Participant panorama)

[Organization Layer Guidance]
14. Let's walk through who would be involved:
    - Who would approve? (Club owner, venue management)
    - Who would manage execution? (IT director, operations director)
    - Which departments would participate? (Ticketing, security, media)
    - Who would use daily? (Audience, athletes, staff)
    - Who else cares? (Sponsors, media, fan community)

[Four-Party Interest Guidance]
15. Think about these four aspects:
    - Who would benefit? (Audience experience, operations efficiency)
    - Whose work would change? (Staff operations)
    - Who would actively support? (IT lead, operations lead)
    - Who might have concerns? (Cost)

[External Connection Guidance]
16. Besides internal staff, external parties?
    - Audience/fan requirements? (Smooth sharing, interactive experience)
    - Sponsor needs? (Brand exposure, data insights)
    - Media connectivity needs?

17. What would each expect?
    [Follow-up] Audience cares about? (Network smooth, social sharing)
    [Follow-up] Athletes/performers care about? (Data collection, performance analysis)
    [Follow-up] Management cares about? (Commercial revenue, fan satisfaction)
