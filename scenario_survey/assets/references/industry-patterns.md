# Industry Purchase Factor Patterns

## Purpose

This document provides industry-specific patterns for purchase factors and success criteria based on scenario_modeler synthesis. Use this to guide pre-sales questionnaire customization for each industry.

---

## Industry Overview

Based on cross-case analysis, the following industries are covered:

| Industry | Key Focus Areas | Typical Stakeholder Categories |
|----------|-----------------|----------------------------|
| Hospitality | Guest experience, multi-property consistency, Wi-Fi coverage | Decision Maker, IT Lead, Guests, Operations |
| Healthcare | Patient data security, remote access, compliance | Decision Maker, IT Lead, Clinical Staff, Regulators |
| Education | Student experience, device coverage, campus connectivity | Decision Maker, IT Lead, Teachers, Students, Parents |
| Logistics | Supply chain continuity, global operations, security | Decision Maker, IT Lead, Operations, Clients |
| Retail | Customer service, logistics efficiency, ratings | Decision Maker, IT Lead, Staff, Customers |
| Manufacturing | Production efficiency, predictive maintenance, compliance | Decision Maker, IT Lead, Operations, Clients |
| SportsEntertainment | Audience experience, data insights, peak capacity | Decision Maker, IT Lead, Operations, Audience |
| Services | Professional efficiency, client collaboration, security | Decision Maker, IT Lead, Professionals, Clients |

## Hospitality Industry Patterns

### Typical Purchase Factors (Priority Order)

| Priority | Factor | Typical Expression | Target Expectation |
|----------|--------|-------------------|--------------------|
| 1 | Guest Wi-Fi experience | "Wi-Fi is fundamental like water/coffee" | Consistent coverage, complaint reduction |
| 2 | Multi-property consistency | "Guest experience should not differ wildly" | Unified standard across all properties |
| 3 | Remote management efficiency | "Don't need to fly to properties for issues" | Centralized management, faster resolution |
| 4 | WAN cost optimization | "Expensive MPLS contracts" | Cost reduction target |
| 5 | Rating/reputation impact | "Guests complimenting connectivity" | Rating improvement |

### Key Stakeholder Expectations

| Stakeholder | Typical Expectation | Priority |
|-------------|--------------------| ---------|
| IT Director | Operational agility, single pane of glass | High |
| Guests | Wi-Fi as basic commodity, consistent quality | High |
| Property managers | No IT expertise needed for daily operations | Medium |
| Finance | WAN cost reduction | Medium |

### Success Criteria Questions

- What guest Wi-Fi complaints currently exist? What reduction target?
- How many properties? What consistency target across locations?
- How is IT currently managing remote properties? What efficiency improvement expected?
- Current WAN costs? What reduction target?
